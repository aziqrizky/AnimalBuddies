About Animal Buddies

A simple and colourful learning website made for young children.

🐼

Our Purpose 🎓

Animal Buddies is a web-based tutorial that helps children learn simple information about animals. The website uses colourful designs, simple words, pictures using emojis, interactive buttons and a quiz to make learning more enjoyable.

👦 Target Users

Children around 5 to 6 years old who are beginning to learn about animals.

📚 Learning Style

Children can read, click, explore and answer questions while learning.

What Makes It Fun? 🎨

🌈

Bold Colours

Bright colours help make the website attractive and friendly.

🖱️

Interaction

Buttons allow children to interact with learning content.

🏆

Quiz

Children can test what they have learned with a simple quiz.
