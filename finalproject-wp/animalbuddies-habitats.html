Where Do Animals Live? 🌍

Animals live in different places called habitats.

🌳
🌳🦁

Land

Land animals live on the ground or in trees.

Examples: Lion, elephant, dog, monkey.

🌊🐬

Water

Water animals live in rivers, lakes or the sea.

Examples: Fish and dolphin.

☁️🦜

Air

Flying animals move through the air.

Examples: Parrot, eagle and butterfly.
